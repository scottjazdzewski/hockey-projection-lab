# Goals Model EXP-0027 — 60-Game Team-Form Window

**Model ID:** `HOCKEY-LAB-GOALS-TEAM-FORM-60-009-RESEARCH`  
**Status:** CANDIDATE for nested window-selection testing; not promoted  
**Run date:** 2026-11-03  
**Champion protection:** Hockey Lab Game v1.0.0 remains frozen. No public export or website change.

## Question and design

EXP-0026 found that 40 prior team-game dates modestly improved expected goals and regulation score probabilities versus the existing 20-date window. EXP-0027 tests a 60-date team/opponent history while holding the league-scoring window at 20 dates. The feature set, opponent-adjusted ratings, Poisson regularization grid, score-law candidates, and rolling-origin protocol are otherwise unchanged.

For target season S, training uses seasons through S−2, alpha and score law are selected on S−1, the model is refit through S−1, and S is scored. The five target seasons are 2022–2026, with 1,312 regular-season games each and 6,560 out-of-fold games total. Same-date results are excluded from pregame features. No market, win-model, or same-game goalie/lineup inputs are used. Canonical winner and regulation-tie/overtime checks passed.

## Results

The score-family choice was diagonal-inflated Poisson in all five validation folds, with the same five fold-specific multipliers as the 20- and 40-date models (1.45, 1.45, 1.60, 1.30, 1.30). Therefore the comparison isolates the team-history window and selected Poisson regularization.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Home regulation-goal MAE | 1.39765 | 1.39605 | 1.39548 |
| Away regulation-goal MAE | 1.35111 | 1.35073 | 1.34878 |

| Regulation score log loss | 3.83752 | 3.83407 | 3.83180 |
| Final score log loss | 3.71356 | 3.71007 | 3.70791 |
| Final-total log loss | 2.17984 | 2.17863 | 2.17814 |
| Regulation 3-way Brier | 0.62564 | 0.62421 | 0.62306 |

| Overtime Brier | 0.173195 | 0.173203 | 0.173080 |
| Full-game home-win Brier from score model | 0.23670 | 0.23590 | 0.23536 |

Versus the 20-date model, the 60-date model improves home/away expected-goal MAE by 0.00217/0.00233, regulation score log loss by 0.00571, and regulation Brier by 0.00259. Final-total underprediction narrows from −0.186 to −0.168 goals per game. The gains are small in expected-goal MAE but consistent in direction with longer team-history smoothing.

The 60-date model improves regulation score log loss and three-way Brier in four of five seasons; the 2026 fold is slightly worse on both (score log loss +0.00007, Brier +0.00072 versus 20 dates). Goal MAE improves in four of five seasons for each side. Overtime Brier changes by only −0.00012; predicted overtime frequency is 21.51% against 22.24% observed.

### Back-to-back diagnostic

Across 1,722 back-to-back games, regulation score log loss improves from 3.85758 (20 dates) to 3.84895 (60 dates), and three-way Brier improves from 0.61882 to 0.61540. In the 2026 fold's 358 back-to-back games, both remain slightly worse than the 20-date model: score log loss 3.80865 versus 3.80498 and Brier 0.64879 versus 0.64720. The 60-date window reduces the 40-date model's larger 2026 B2B regression, but does not remove it.

## Decision and limitation

Keep the 60-date model at **CANDIDATE** for nested window-selection testing. Do not promote or publish it. The 20/40/60 architecture comparison uses the same five development seasons, and the 60-date choice followed review of the 40-date result; these scores are not an untouched promotion test. EXP-0028 will select among 20/40/60 separately within each preceding validation season, then score the next season. A later promotion still requires a forward/shadow test and calibration review. Hockey Lab Game v1.0.0 remains unchanged.
